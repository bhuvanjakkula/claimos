⚖️ Carmack Statutory Framework

Under the Carmack Amendment (49 U.S.C. § 14706), motor carriers are strictly liable for the actual loss or injury to property caused by the carrier. A prima facie claim requires establishing:

  • Delivery to carrier at origin in good condition (origin pulp ≤ spec)
  • Arrival at destination in damaged or deteriorated condition
  • Specific quantifiable damages (declared value of affected cargo)

ClaimOS enforces the 9-month notice of claim deadline and requires a formal 48-hour demand for the reefer micro-controller download before memory buffers overwrite.

🛡️ The "Dock-Clean Trap" Countermeasure

The #1 defense carriers exploit to deny refrigerated claims is the Dock-Clean Trap:

A trailer reefer unit fails mid-route, causing cargo to spike to 12.8 °C. Before arriving at the destination, the unit is restarted, cooling the cargo back into range (6.4 °C) by delivery time. Standard handoff pulp checks report "in-spec", causing shippers to unknowingly forfeit claims.

ClaimOS continuously analyzes logger time-series telemetry to detect hidden in-transit excursions regardless of receiver dock temperature.
